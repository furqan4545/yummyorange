Save TikTok videos on a Mac

Drag a TikTok video’s address out of your browser, press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP4 or MP3. YummyOrange saves it in Downloads, named after the video. Free and open source, on your Mac.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to save a TikTok video on a Mac

  1. Open the video on tiktok.com in your browser.
  2. Press on the address in the address bar and drag it out (a shared vm.tiktok.com or vt.tiktok.com link works too).
  3. Press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP4 for the video or MP3 for the sound.
  4. The file appears in Downloads.

Good to know

  • The first time, YummyOrange asks before downloading yt-dlp, the free, open-source downloader it uses (about 37 MB), from yt-dlp’s own GitHub page, and keeps it only if it matches yt-dlp’s published checksum.
  • For a post that only signed-in visitors can see, YummyOrange asks, every time, before using your sign-in from the browser you dragged the link from (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Vivaldi or Opera).
  • Nothing goes through a website or server of ours: the video comes straight from the site to your Mac.
  • Please download only your own posts, or posts whose owner allows it.

Questions

Do short share links work?

Yes: tiktok.com/@name/video/…, tiktok.com/t/…, vm.tiktok.com and vt.tiktok.com links.

Can I save only the sound?

Yes. Let go on MP3, M4A or WAV.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
