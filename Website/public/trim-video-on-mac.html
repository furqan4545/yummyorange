Trim a video on a Mac

Drag a video, press ⌘ Command and let go on Trim…: a window with a film strip and in and out handles makes exact, frame-accurate cuts, saved in the file’s own format next to the original. Free and open source, on your Mac.

How to trim a video on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Start dragging the video (or a sound file) and press ⌘ Command.
  3. Let go on Trim…. Move the in and out handles on the film strip (a waveform for sound).
  4. Save. The trimmed copy is saved beside the original.

More video editing

  • Split…: put splits on a timeline, like a video editor, or split evenly, and save each part.
  • Merge two or more videos or sounds into one file, in the order you dropped them.
  • Crop…, Speed… (0.25× to 4×, pitch kept), Mute, Redact… (blur or black boxes for a time range), Snapshot… of an exact frame.

Questions

Is the cut exact?

Yes, Trim makes frame-accurate cuts in the file’s own format.

Does it work for audio?

Yes. Sound files open with a waveform instead of a film strip.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
