Convert MOV to MP4 on a Mac

Drag a MOV (a screen recording, an iPhone video), press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP4: YummyOrange saves an MP4 next to it, on your Mac. Free and open source, with nothing uploaded.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to convert MOV to MP4 on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Start dragging the video and press ⇧ Shift.
  3. Let go on MP4. “name.mp4” is saved beside the original.

More you can do with a video

  • Convert to MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, GIF, or just its sound as MP3, M4A or WMA.
  • With ⌘ Command: Compress or Compress to… a size, Trim…, Split…, Crop…, Speed…, Mute, Extract Audio, Snapshot… of a frame.
  • Merge two or more videos into one file.

Questions

Do I need ffmpeg?

YummyOrange uses ffmpeg for video. If your Mac doesn’t have it, YummyOrange downloads its own copy from yummyorange.com once (about 12 MB) and uses that.

Is the original changed?

No. The MP4 is a new file next to the MOV.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
