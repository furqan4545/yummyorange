Compress files on a Mac

Drag a file, press ⌘ Command and let go on Compress, or on Compress to… to hit a size in MB, such as an email attachment limit. YummyOrange saves the smaller copy next to the original, on your Mac, for free.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to compress a file on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Start dragging a PDF, image, video or sound file and press ⌘ Command. The Tools wheel opens.
  3. Let go on Compress, or on Compress to… and type the size you need.
  4. The smaller copy is saved beside the original, which stays as it was.

What can be compressed

  • PDFs: Compress and Compress to….
  • Images: Compress, Compress to…, Half Size and Resize….
  • Video and audio: Compress and Compress to….
  • Folders and groups of files: ZIP, TAR or GZIP from the ⇧ Shift wheel, or Zip from the ⌥ Option wheel.

Questions

Can it make a file fit an email limit?

Yes. Compress to… asks for a size in MB and makes the copy fit it.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Compression happens on your Mac.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
