Convert a PDF to Word on a Mac

Drag the PDF, press ⇧ Shift and let go on DOCX: YummyOrange saves an editable Word document next to the PDF. It’s free and open source, works on your Mac with nothing uploaded, and never changes the original.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to convert a PDF to Word on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Start dragging the PDF and press ⇧ Shift. The Convert wheel opens with the formats a PDF can become.
  3. Let go on DOCX.
  4. “name.docx” is saved beside the PDF, ready to open in Word or Pages.

Everything a PDF can become

  • DOCX (Word), EPUB, PPTX and XLSX.
  • TXT and Markdown: the text reflowed, with text recognition (OCR) for scanned pages.
  • PNG, JPG and SVG images of its pages.
  • Several PDFs at once: select them all and drag them together.
  • The other way round too: Word (DOCX, DOC), RTF, HTML, TXT, Markdown, ODT and Pages documents convert to PDF.

Questions

Is the Word file editable?

Yes. The text comes across as editable text. A very complex layout may need tidying in Word or Pages.

Does it upload my PDF?

No. The conversion runs on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.

Is it free?

Yes. YummyOrange is free and open source under the MIT licence.
