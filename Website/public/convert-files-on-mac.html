Convert any file on your Mac

YummyOrange converts images, video, audio, PDFs, documents, ebooks, slides, spreadsheets and subtitles on your Mac. Drag a file, press ⇧ Shift and drop it on the format you want; the new file is saved next to the original, which is never changed. It’s free and open source, and nothing is uploaded.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to convert a file on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Start dragging one file, or several, and press ⇧ Shift. The Convert wheel shows the formats your files can become.
  3. Let go on a format. The new files are saved next to the originals as “name.ext” (or “name 2.ext” if that name is taken).

Questions

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Select them and drag them together. For a mix of kinds, the wheel offers the formats every file can reach.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. Audio and video use ffmpeg; if your Mac doesn’t have it, YummyOrange downloads its own copy from yummyorange.com once (about 12 MB). Saving pictures as WebP works the same way.

Does it work offline?

Yes, everything except downloads from links runs on your Mac with no internet.
