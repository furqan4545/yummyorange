Convert a video to MP3 on a Mac

Drag a video, press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP3: YummyOrange saves the sound as an MP3 next to the video. With ⌘ Command, Extract Audio copies the sound out without re-encoding when it can. Free and open source, on your Mac.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to convert a video to MP3 on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Start dragging the video and press ⇧ Shift.
  3. Let go on MP3 (or M4A, WMA). The sound is saved beside the video.

More ways to get the sound

  • From a link: drag a YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok or Threads address out of the browser, press ⇧ Shift, let go on MP3, M4A or WAV.
  • Normalize evens out the loudness; Channels… makes it mono or stereo, or swaps sides.
  • Convert sound files between MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, AIFF and WMA.

Questions

Which is better, MP3 or Extract Audio?

Extract Audio copies AAC sound (most MP4 and MOV videos) into an M4A without re-encoding, so nothing is lost, and saves any other sound as MP3. The MP3 petal always makes an MP3, for players that need one.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
