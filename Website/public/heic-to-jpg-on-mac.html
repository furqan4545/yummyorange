Convert HEIC photos to JPG on a Mac

iPhone photos arrive as HEIC. Drag one or many, press ⇧ Shift and let go on JPG: YummyOrange saves JPG copies next to the originals, on your Mac, for free.

How to convert HEIC to JPG on a Mac

  1. Open YummyOrange from the menu bar.
  2. Select the HEIC photos in Finder and start dragging them. Press ⇧ Shift.
  3. Let go on JPG. A JPG copy of each photo is saved beside it; the HEIC files stay as they were.

More you can do with photos

  • Convert to PNG, WebP, TIFF, AVIF, GIF, BMP or PDF, or PPTX with one slide each.
  • With ⌘ Command: Compress or Compress to… a size in MB, Resize…, Half Size, Crop…, Rotate 90°.
  • Strip Metadata saves a copy without camera and location details.
  • Make PDF or Collage from several photos; Add Background… puts a photo on a wallpaper, gradient or colour.

Questions

Can I convert a whole batch?

Yes. Select all the photos and drag them together; each gets its own JPG.

Are the originals kept?

Yes. YummyOrange never changes the file you drop; the JPG is a new file next to it.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
