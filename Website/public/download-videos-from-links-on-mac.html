Save videos from YouTube, X and Threads on a Mac

YummyOrange saves videos, or just their sound, from links to YouTube, X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok and Threads. Drag the address out of your browser, press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP4, MP3, M4A or WAV; the file lands in Downloads. Free and open source, on your Mac.

Download YummyOrange

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · about 7 MB

How to save a video from a link on a Mac

  1. Open the video in your browser: a YouTube video or Short, a post on X, a Threads post.
  2. Press on the address in the address bar and drag it out (or drag a link or thumbnail from a page).
  3. Press ⇧ Shift. The Convert wheel opens with MP4, MP3, M4A and WAV.
  4. Let go on a format. The file is saved in Downloads, named after the post.

Which links work

  • YouTube: youtube.com/watch, Shorts, live and youtu.be links.
  • X (Twitter): x.com and twitter.com posts with a video.
  • Threads: threads.com and threads.net posts; these need nothing extra.
  • Instagram and TikTok: see their own guides.
  • The first time, YummyOrange asks before downloading yt-dlp, the free, open-source downloader it uses (about 37 MB), from yt-dlp’s own GitHub page, and keeps it only if it matches yt-dlp’s published checksum.
  • For a post that only signed-in visitors can see, YummyOrange asks, every time, before using your sign-in from the browser you dragged the link from (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Vivaldi or Opera).
  • Nothing goes through a website or server of ours: the video comes straight from the site to your Mac.
  • Download only your own videos, or videos whose owner allows it; YouTube’s terms allow downloads only where YouTube offers them.

Questions

Can I save a YouTube video as MP3?

Yes. Drag its address, press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP3.

Where does the file go?

Into your Downloads folder, named after the post. It also shows up on YummyOrange’s shelf or Clothesline, like any new file.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
