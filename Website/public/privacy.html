Privacy policy

Last changed 25 November 2026

YummyOrange works on your files where they are, on your Mac. It doesn’t collect, send or sell anything about you or your files.

The app

  • Your files stay on your Mac. YummyOrange reads a file in its own folder and writes the result beside it. Nothing is uploaded, and document importers never fetch anything from the network.
  • Update checks. The app’s only network requests of its own ask this website (yummyorange.com) whether a new version of YummyOrange is out (the update information and its signature), a few times a day, and download it when you choose to install it. The requests name the app and its version; their other headers are the same for every copy (the language is always English), and they carry no identifier, no cookies and nothing about your files. Like any request they reach the website’s host from your address (see below). When an update is installed, macOS also checks the new version’s signing certificate with Apple, as it does for any signed app. Settings › General can turn the automatic check off.
  • No account and no analytics. There is nothing to sign up for, and the app has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code.
  • What it keeps. Your settings, stored by macOS for YummyOrange on your Mac. The Clothesline keeps a list of the files hung on it (it only points to them), and copied screenshots you choose to hang are saved in Pictures › Clothesline. Nothing of this leaves your Mac.
  • When you send something. AirDrop and Move To do what you ask with the files you pick, through macOS.
  • Permissions. macOS asks before YummyOrange first saves into your Desktop, Documents or Downloads folder or onto an external drive, and before it reads the first copied screenshot. Accessibility access is needed only for the optional Hold the key first. Update notices arrive quietly in Notification Center, without a permission prompt; Settings › Notifications can turn them off or into banners.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies: its type, pictures and scripts all come from this site.
  • The try-it demos run entirely in your browser. Nothing you drag on the page is sent anywhere.
  • Like any web server, the one that hosts these pages may keep short-lived access logs (the address a request came from, the time and the page). They are used only to keep the site running.
