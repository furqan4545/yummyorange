Release notes

YummyOrange updates itself: it lets you know when a new version is out, and Check for Updates… in its menu-bar menu looks right away. Each version is listed here, and the newest is always on the home page too. Your settings stay.

YummyOrange 1.0.7

26 November 2026

  • Edit PDF: light text on a dark background in a scan (a white heading on a coloured band) now keeps its colour when you edit it, and the old words are covered properly.
  • Edit PDF: italic text in a scan is matched to an italic font, so edited lines keep their look.
  • Edit PDF: a colour picked in the Colors window now stays on the line.

YummyOrange 1.0.6

26 November 2026

  • Sign PDFs. In Edit PDF, Sign › Draw Signature… lets you sign with your trackpad or mouse; your signature is kept on your Mac for next time. Drag it into place and drag its corner to resize it.
  • Pictures and stamps. Sign › Add Picture or Stamp… puts any picture on the page, transparent edges included.
  • Highlight. Choose Highlight and drag across text, in yellow, green, blue or pink. It works on scanned pages too.
  • Font, bold, italic and colour. Select any line to change its font, make it bold or italic, or change its size and colour. Added text gets bold and italic too.

YummyOrange 1.0.5

26 November 2026

  • Edit PDF works on scanned pages. On a scan with recognized text, a line you change now changes on the page: the old words are covered in the paper’s own colour and the new ones are drawn in the closest matching font, size and colour. Words you don’t change keep their original look, and Delete removes a line from the page.

YummyOrange 1.0.4

26 November 2026

  • Nothing to install for audio and video. If your Mac has no ffmpeg, YummyOrange downloads its own (about 11 MB, once) and keeps it across updates. Settings › About shows which ffmpeg is in use.
  • Redact’s blur, Visualize’s spectrum and AV1 videos (on Macs with M3 or later) work with YummyOrange’s ffmpeg too.

YummyOrange 1.0.3

25 November 2026

  • YummyOrange now looks for a new version every hour, not every six, so you hear about updates sooner.

YummyOrange 1.0.2

25 November 2026

  • Settings › About shows the YummyOrange icon and © 2026 Epoxlab LLC.
  • New copies start with the Pop petal sound and haptic feedback off; your own choices stay as they are.

YummyOrange 1.0.1

25 November 2026

  • Talk to the Founder… in the menu-bar menu and in Settings › About: write to Ali, who makes YummyOrange, or copy his address.
  • Updates you won’t miss. A notification when a new version is out, or the update window if notifications are off, and a dot on the menu-bar icon while it waits. Check for Updates… is in Settings › General too. It waits while you present or watch something full screen.
  • ⌥ Option is the Actions key. ⌃ still opens Actions mid-drag, and keys you chose yourself in Settings stay as they are.
  • A faster menu. The menu-bar menu opens at once; work it used to do on every open now happens in the background.
  • The Settings window is titled with the pane you’re on.

YummyOrange 1.0.0

25 November 2026 · The first release

  • Three wheels. Mid-drag, ⇧ opens Convert, ⌘ opens Tools and ⌥ opens Actions, each with only the petals that fit the files you’re dragging.
  • Convert images, video, audio, PDFs, documents, ebooks and Pages files, slides, spreadsheets and subtitles; pack folders as ZIP, TAR or GZIP.
  • Tools such as Compress, Resize, Rotate, Strip Metadata, Extract Text, Read QR Code, Mute, Normalize, Split Pages, Add Password and Extract, plus editor windows for Trim, Split, Crop, Redact, Adjust, Annotate, Snapshot, Bleep, Speed, Channels, Visualize, Organize and Edit PDF.
  • Actions for any file or folder: Copy, Copy Path, Reveal, Terminal, AirDrop, Move To, Zip and Float.
  • New files open on a shelf beside the pointer, on the Clothesline under the notch, in Finder, or nowhere, as you choose. Copied screenshots can hang on the Clothesline too.
  • A short welcome tour with a sample photo to practise on, light and dark appearance, and Reduce Motion.
