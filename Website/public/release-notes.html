Release notes

YummyOrange updates itself: it lets you know when a new version is out, and Check for Updates… in its menu-bar menu looks right away. Each version is listed here, and the newest is always on the home page too. Your settings stay.

YummyOrange 1.0.0

Not released yetThe first release

  • Three wheels. Mid-drag, ⇧ opens Convert, ⌘ opens Tools and ⌃ or ⌥ opens Actions, each with only the petals that fit the files you’re dragging.
  • Convert images, video, audio, PDFs, documents, ebooks and Pages files, slides, spreadsheets and subtitles; pack folders as ZIP, TAR or GZIP.
  • Tools such as Compress, Resize, Rotate, Strip Metadata, Extract Text, Read QR Code, Mute, Normalize, Split Pages, Add Password and Extract, plus editor windows for Trim, Split, Crop, Redact, Adjust, Annotate, Snapshot, Bleep, Speed, Channels, Visualize, Organize and Edit PDF.
  • Actions for any file or folder: Copy, Copy Path, Reveal, Terminal, AirDrop, Move To, Zip and Float.
  • New files open on a shelf beside the pointer, on the Clothesline under the notch, in Finder, or nowhere, as you choose. Copied screenshots can hang on the Clothesline too.
  • A short welcome tour with a sample photo to practise on, light and dark appearance, and Reduce Motion.
