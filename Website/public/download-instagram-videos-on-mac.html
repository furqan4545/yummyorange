Save Instagram videos on a Mac

Drag an Instagram post’s address out of your browser, press ⇧ Shift and let go on MP4 for the video or MP3 for its sound. YummyOrange saves the file in Downloads, named after the post. Free and open source, on your Mac.

How to save an Instagram video on a Mac

  1. Open the Reel or post in Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox or another browser.
  2. Press on the address in the address bar and drag it out. A link or thumbnail on a page works too.
  3. Press ⇧ Shift. The Convert wheel opens with MP4, MP3, M4A and WAV.
  4. Let go on MP4 for the video, or MP3 for the sound. It downloads into Downloads.

Good to know

  • The first time, YummyOrange asks before downloading yt-dlp, the free, open-source downloader it uses (about 37 MB), from yt-dlp’s own GitHub page, and keeps it only if it matches yt-dlp’s published checksum.
  • For a post that only signed-in visitors can see, YummyOrange asks, every time, before using your sign-in from the browser you dragged the link from (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Vivaldi or Opera).
  • Nothing goes through a website or server of ours: the video comes straight from the site to your Mac.
  • Please download only your own posts, or posts whose owner allows it.

Questions

Does it work with Reels?

Yes: Reels, video posts and IGTV links (instagram.com/reel/…, /p/…, /tv/…).

Can I get just the audio?

Yes. Let go on MP3, M4A or WAV instead of MP4.

Do I need an Instagram account?

Not for public posts. For posts only signed-in visitors see, YummyOrange can use your browser’s sign-in, and asks first.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
